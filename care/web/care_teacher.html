Press start. Each posture has a short get-ready time, then it records.

Placement checklist

  1. Mount the camera once, about 1.5–2 m high, so the whole room including the bed is in view. Then press Lock this view.
  2. Record 3 rounds (different times of day) as train, and 1 round on another day as test.
  3. Do not move the camera or the sensors afterwards. If the page says the view changed, re-lock and re-record that round.
  4. Everyone in view should know they are being watched during set-up. Close this page when finished; the camera is only a teacher.
